What is Ziting

Institutional demo / research lab · Decree 2026-008

Ziting builds a lawful, auditable, AI-administered civic entity in the Eastern Roman (Byzantine) administrative tradition. It is not a costume club, not a militia, and not a claim to replace or impersonate any sovereign state.

What it is. Written law, open archives, transparent dues, and a civil service modeled on Byzantine offices (Basileus, logothetes, quaestor, sakellarios, and others) as role blueprints—not as recognized imperial status. Software agents run routine admin (registry, dues, small claims, decree logs, budgets); humans keep constitutional veto. Progress is scored only on a public ladder:

  • L0 Law & symbols
  • L1 Digital administration
  • L2 Host-law jurisdictional foothold
  • L3 Formal international writing or a documented de facto case study

“AI-run polity” means ≥ L1. Day-to-day state-building goes out under the office of Basileus; private identities stay off this channel.

What it is not. No arms or combat gear (defense = text/tabletop only, if ever). No fake UN seats, borders, or police powers beyond voluntary membership and host-law contracts. No gray funds. Not a personal influencer brand—separate books and seals.

Read Ziting as institutional R&D under real-world law. Fail L2 honestly → stay a cloud republic + Roman-law workshop. Do not cosplay L3.

Roads, laws, icons—administration first; purple on the map later.
